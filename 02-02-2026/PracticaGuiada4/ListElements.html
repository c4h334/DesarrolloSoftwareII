Recipe for the best pancakes

Ingredients

For ingredients:

  • A cup of flour
  • A cup of milk
  • An egg
  • A tablespoon sugar
  • A teaspoon baking powder

Instructions:

  1. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Heat a pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour the batter into the pan.
  4. Cook until golden Brown and serve.
